# Doctor - Symbiants: best-in-slot AND where to farm them

A Doctor fills all 13 implant slots with SUPPORT-Unit symbiants. This is read from the client data, not from memory: every Support symbiant's ToWear action carries Profession(stat 60) EqualTo locks {2 MartialArtist, 4 Fixer, 6 Adventurer, 7 Trader, 10 Doctor, 12 Meta-Physicist, 14 Keeper} - Doctor is in the Support set and in NO other unit line (Artillery = Soldier/Fixer/Agent/Adventurer/Trader, Control = Engineer/Trader/Bureaucrat/MP, Extermination = Bureaucrat/NT/MP, Infantry = MA/Adventurer/Enforcer/Keeper). Of the 300 symbiant templates in items.ocp that a Doctor can wear at all, 285 are Support-line; the other 15 are the known unlocked-Artillery client-data anomaly (ids 278908-278919) plus a few unlined one-offs, listed under outsideLineSymbiants.

Support carries exactly the Doctor's stat sheet: Biological Metamorphosis and Matter Metamorphosis (the heal schools), Matter Creation, Treatment and First Aid, Nano Pool / Max Nano / Nano Init, plus Body Dev and Max Health so the raid's heal target survives being focused. That is why symbiants beat cluster implants for a Doctor from the mid-game on.

Best-in-slot at endgame is the Xan Support-Unit ALPHA in every slot - e.g. the Brain Alpha (id 278996) gives Bio Met +144, Matter Met +144, Matter Creation +144, First Aid +144 and Comp. Lit. +144. Progression: wear the Xan Support Beta first (lower requirements), then swap each slot to the crafted Alpha once Treatment, the abilities and level 219 allow.

WHERE TO GET THEM: the Support ladder up to QL280 is Shadowlands pocket-boss loot (summon the boss at an Incarnator from a crafted Novictalized Notum Crystal); the QL300 'Intelligent ... Support Unit Aban' for every slot comes from The Night Heart on Pandemonium Antenora; the Xan Betas drop in the three Legacy of the Xan instances off the Central Hub (Ear and Thigh are quest rewards instead); the Alpha is crafted. Per-item farm detail is in acquisitionBySlot and farmingRuns - ? of ? catalog entries carry a specific cited location, 67 are left unknown rather than guessed.

**Farm coverage:** acquisition verified for **218 of 285** Support-unit symbiants usable by a Doctor; **67** are left `unknown` rather than guessed.

## Which symbiant line?

- **Recommended unit: Support** - Support is the ONLY unit line a Doctor can wear. Verified from items.ocp: every Support symbiant's ToWear action profession-locks to stat 60 EqualTo {2,4,6,7,10,12,14} = MartialArtist, Fixer, Adventurer, Trader, Doctor, Meta-Physicist, Keeper. Doctor (10) appears in no other line's lock set. This is not a preference - there is no alternative unit to choose.

## Farming methods

**pocketBoss:** Pocket boss: farm the four pattern pieces (Aban/Abhan, Bhotaar, Chi, Dom) from the listed mobs, combine them in order into a Complete Blueprint Pattern, combine that with a Crystal Filled by the Source (Nano Programming 6x QL), novictalize it with Subdued Flow of Novictum via an Ancient Novictum Refiner (Quantum FT 4.5x QL), then right-click the crystal on an Incarnator whose QL is >= the boss QL. Incarnator QL caps: Nascense 75 | Elysium South 115 / North 130 | Scheol South 140 / North 160 | Adonis City East 170 / West 190 | Penumbra West (The Pipe) 200 / East (Fortress) 215 | Inferno South (Barracks) 225 / North (Frontier) 255 | Pandemonium 270.

## Alpha crafting

**what:** Xan Alpha symbiants are the most advanced symbiants in AO. They cannot be looted - each is crafted by the player from two QL300 components, and the result is NODROP (items.ocp Flags bit 26, verified) and cannot be reverted.

**recipe:** Combine a 'Xan <slot> Symbiant, Support Unit Beta' with an 'Intelligent <slot> Symbiant, Support Unit Aban' QL300 of the SAME slot and the SAME unit line. No tradeskill tool or nano is required.

**components:**
- xanBeta: Xan Support-Unit Beta for the slot. Per the Legacy-of-the-Xan encounter guides: Mitaar Hero (Technomaster Sinuh) drops Brain, Chest, Left Hand and Left Wrist; LoX: Ground Chief Vortexx drops Ocular, Left Arm, Right Wrist and Waist; The Xan 12-man (Deranged Xan) drops Right Arm, Right Hand and Feet - all three entered from floor portals in the Central Hub. Ear and Thigh are quest rewards (Arid Rift / Neretva Canyons Area X) and are the only two NODROP Betas.
- intelligentQL300: 'Intelligent <slot> Symbiant, Support Unit Aban' QL300 - the top-QL Shadowlands symbiant for the slot. The recorded source for all 13 is The Night Heart on Pandemonium Antenora (level-250 static raid boss; it is NOT a pocket boss and cannot be summoned at an Incarnator). Tradeable, so it can also be bought.

**equipRequirements:**
- note: Requirements to WEAR the finished Support Alpha, read from items.ocp ToWear.
- Treatment: 2088
- level: 219
- abilitiesPerSlot: OrderedDict({'Head/Brain': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Intelligence'], 'Eye/Ocular': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Strength'], 'Ear': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Intelligence'], 'Chest': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Stamina'], 'Left Arm': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Psychic'], 'Left Hand': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Psychic'], 'Left Wrist': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Intelligence'], 'Right Arm': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Strength'], 'Right Hand': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Strength'], 'Right Wrist': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Psychic'], 'Waist': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Strength'], 'Legs/Thigh': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Intelligence'], 'Feet': ['Agility', 'Sense', 'Stamina']})
- abilityValue: 1276
- fixedAcrossEverySlot: ['Agility', 'Sense']
- differenceFromControl: Note for anyone porting the Meta-Physicist page: the Control Alpha's two fixed abilities are Intelligence + Psychic. The Support Alpha's are different - see fixedAcrossEverySlot, read per slot from the item data.

**assembleRequirements:**
- note: Sources disagree on whether the combine itself needs a skill: the ao-universe Alpha-symbiant tradeskill guide lists Treatment 2088 / abilities 1276 (the same numbers as the finished Alpha's EQUIP requirements read from items.ocp), while the Athen Paladins guide says 'no skill is required'. UNRESOLVED - recorded in _unverified, not asserted either way.

**clickyTrick:** The Xan Beta Ear and Thigh are the quest rewards and the only two that are clickable: right-click cycles their unit type through all five lines. The other eleven Betas are not clickable - their unit is fixed on drop. Money-saver: buy the cheapest Intelligent Ear/Thigh of any unit, cycle the Beta to match, combine, then cycle the resulting Alpha back to Support.

## Verification

- **namesAndIds:** All 39 ids in bestInSlot (13 Alpha + 13 Intelligent QL300 + 13 Beta components) come from items.ocp and were name-verified against itemnames.sql by the extractor.
- **statsAndReqs:** QL, keyBonuses and reqs extracted from items.ocp (OMNICELL-CONTENT v3, client 18.8.50_EP1) with tools/mp-symbiant-extractor run as --prof 10 --slug doctor; stat numbers mapped from AOSharp.Common Stat.cs; ToWear = equip requirements, wear-modifier functions (target=2) = stat bonuses. GreaterThan is rendered as value+1 (the runtime meaning is stat >= value+1).
- **professionLock:** Support unit ToWear stat-60 EqualTo {2,4,6,7,10,12,14} => MartialArtist / Fixer / Adventurer / Trader / Doctor / Meta-Physicist / Keeper. Doctor appears in no other line.
- **noDropFlags:** NODROP/Unique read from the client data, not a website: items.ocp Stats entry 0 ("Flags"), bit 26 = NoDrop, bit 27 = Unique. The per-record flags int in the pack is 0 for every template and is not used.
- **expansionGate:** ToWear stat 389 BitAnd: bit 2 = Shadowlands, bit 128 = Legacy of the Xan. Every Xan Alpha/Beta carries bit 128.
- **farmLocations:** Acquisition is joined onto every item by AO item id from (1) the TinkerTools/TinkerPocket community drop database via its public API and (2) hand-curated entries in tools/mp-symbiant-extractor/farm-sources-manual.json for the Legacy-of-the-Xan Betas, the crafted Alphas and the one-off symbiants, each with its own guide URL. That farm file is profession-independent and was built once for all classes; it was REUSED here, not re-fetched.
- **bestInSlotIsDerived:** bestInSlot is generated from the dump, not typed by hand: for each slot it takes the Support-line Xan Alpha and reads its bonuses and ToWear requirements directly. No bonus number in it was transcribed.
