# Engineer - Symbiants: best-in-slot AND where to farm them

A top Engineer fills all 13 implant slots with CONTROL-Unit symbiants - the nano/pet-caster unit. The Control line is profession-locked to Engineer, Trader, Bureaucrat and Meta-Physicist (verified: every Control symbiant's ToWear action carries a stat-60 EqualTo lock to {3 Engineer, 7 Trader, 8 Bureaucrat, 12 Meta-Physicist} in items.ocp). IMPORTANT ENGINEER-SPECIFIC FACT, verified from the client data and NOT the same as the Meta-Physicist: Control is the ONLY unit line an Engineer can wear. Running the extractor with --prof 3 returns 300 wearable symbiant templates - 285 Control, 14 client-data anomalies with no profession lock at all (see otherUsableLines) and 1 Engineer-only quest symbiant (see outsideLineSymbiants). An MP may choose between Control, Extermination and Support; an Engineer has no choice to make. Control symbiants stack the six nano schools (Matter Creation, Time & Space, Biological Metamorphosis, Matter Metamorphosis, Psychological Modifications, Sensory Improvement) plus Nano Pool, Max Nano, Nano Init, Treatment, Nano Resist, Nano Programming and Comp. Lit. For an Engineer that is a double win: Matter Creation and Time & Space are the two skills EVERY robot summon requires at the same value (verified in nanos.ocp - every bot nano's ToUse is MaterialCreation > X and SpaceTime > X), and the Treatment / Nano Programming / Comp. Lit. bonuses feed the Engineer's tradeskill role. This is why symbiants outclass cluster implants for an Engineer from the mid-levels on. Best-in-slot at endgame is the Xan Control-Unit ALPHA in every slot (e.g. all six nano schools +144 and +574 Max Nano on the Brain/Head Alpha). Progression: wear the Xan Control Beta first (lower reqs), then swap each slot to the crafted Alpha once Treatment/Int/Psychic/level allow. WHERE TO GET THEM: the Control ladder is Shadowlands pocket-boss loot (summon the boss at an Incarnator from a crafted Novictalized Notum Crystal) up to QL280; the QL300 'Intelligent ... Control Unit Aban' for every slot comes from The Night Heart on Pandemonium Antenora; the Xan Betas come from the three Legacy-of-the-Xan Central Hub encounters (Mitaar Hero, Ground Chief Vortexx, The Xan 12-man) except Ear and Thigh which are quest rewards (Arid Rift / Neretva Canyons Area X); the Alphas are crafted, never looted. See farmingRuns for the location-by-location plan and acquisitionBySlot for the per-slot ladder.

**Farm coverage:** acquisition verified for **228 of 285** Control-unit symbiants usable by a Engineer; **57** are left `unknown` rather than guessed.

## Which symbiant line?

- **Recommended unit: Control** - Control is the caster/pet unit; it carries the nano-school + nano-pool + nano-init bonuses that a robot-summoning Engineer lives on (Matter Creation and Time & Space are both required, at equal value, by every bot summon nano), plus the Treatment / Nano Programming / Comp. Lit. bonuses the Engineer wants as the game's tradeskill profession. Verified: every Control symbiant's ToWear action profession-locks to stat 60 EqualTo {3 Engineer, 7 Trader, 8 Bureaucrat, 12 Meta-Physicist} (items.ocp).
- Also wearable by this profession: Control. There is NO unit choice for an Engineer: Control is the only one of the five unit lines whose stat-60 lock set includes profession 3. Verified by enumerating all 1462 wearable symbiant templates in items.ocp - the five lock sets are Artillery {Soldier, Fixer, Agent, Adventurer, Trader}, Control {Engineer, Trader, Bureaucrat, Meta-Physicist}, Extermination {Bureaucrat, NanoTechnician, Meta-Physicist}, Infantry {MartialArtist, Adventurer, Enforcer, Keeper} and Support {MartialArtist, Fixer, Adventurer, Trader, Doctor, Meta-Physicist, Keeper}. Engineer appears in exactly one of them. This is a genuine difference from the Meta-Physicist, which appears in three.

## Farming methods

**pocketBoss:** Pocket boss: farm the four pattern pieces (Aban/Abhan, Bhotaar, Chi, Dom) from the listed mobs, combine them in order into a Complete Blueprint Pattern, combine that with a Crystal Filled by the Source (Nano Programming 6x QL), novictalize it with Subdued Flow of Novictum via an Ancient Novictum Refiner (Quantum FT 4.5x QL), then right-click the crystal on an Incarnator whose QL is >= the boss QL. Incarnator QL caps: Nascense 75 | Elysium South 115 / North 130 | Scheol South 140 / North 160 | Adonis City East 170 / West 190 | Penumbra West (The Pipe) 200 / East (Fortress) 215 | Inferno South (Barracks) 225 / North (Frontier) 255 | Pandemonium 270.

## Alpha crafting

**what:** Xan Alpha symbiants are the most advanced symbiants in AO. They cannot be looted - each is crafted by the player from two QL300 components and is NODROP / cannot be reverted once assembled.

**recipe:** Combine (drag + shift-click, or the tradeskill window) a Xan <slot> Symbiant, Control Unit Beta with an Intelligent <slot> Symbiant, Control Unit Aban QL300 of the SAME slot and unit. No tradeskill tool or nano is required.

**components:**
- xanBeta: Xan Control-Unit Beta for the slot. Mitaar Hero (Technomaster Sinuh) gives Brain, Chest, Left Hand, Left Wrist; Ground Chief Vortexx gives Ocular, Left Arm, Right Wrist, Waist; The Xan 12-man (Deranged Xan) gives Right Arm, Right Hand, Feet - all three entered from the Central Hub. Ear and Thigh are quest rewards instead (Arid Rift / Neretva Canyons Area X) and are the only two NODROP Betas.
- intelligentQL300: Intelligent Control-Unit (Aban) QL300 for the slot - the top-QL Shadowlands symbiant. Verified: for all 13 Control slots the only recorded source is The Night Heart on Pandemonium Antenora (level-250 static raid boss, NOT a pocket boss - it cannot be summoned at an Incarnator). Tradeable (no NoDrop bit in items.ocp), so it can also be bought.

**assembleRequirements:**
- note: Skill needed to perform the combine (ao-universe Alpha-symbiant tradeskill guide).
- Treatment: 2088
- abilitiesTotal: 1276
- abilityRule: Three abilities: two fixed by unit + one variable by slot. For Control the two fixed are Intelligence + Psychic.

**equipRequirements:**
- note: Requirements to WEAR the finished Control Alpha, read from items.ocp ToWear (identical across all 13 slots except the one variable ability).
- Treatment: 2088
- Intelligence: 1276
- Psychic: 1276
- level: 219
- variableAbilityBySlot: third equip ability is Agility (Head/Ear/Waist/Thigh), Sense (Eye/LeftArm/LeftWrist/Feet), Stamina (Chest/LeftHand) or Strength (RightArm/RightHand/RightWrist), each 1276

**clickyTrick:** Xan Beta Ear and Thigh are the quest rewards and the ONLY two that are clickable: right-click cycles their unit type through all five lines. The other eleven Betas are NOT clickable - their unit is fixed on drop. Money-saver: buy the cheapest Intelligent Ear/Thigh of any unit, cycle the Beta to match, combine, then cycle the resulting Alpha back to Control. (ao-universe / Funcom guide.)

**upgradeOverBase:** Per slot the Alpha beats both components. Head example: Beta nano-schools +130 / Max Nano +519; Intelligent (SL QL300) +137 / +547; Alpha +144 / +574. Alpha is a straight upgrade of the QL300 SL symbiant, with the same equip reqs.

## Verification

- **namesAndIds:** All 39 ids (13 Intelligent + 13 Beta + 13 Alpha) verified in itemnames.sql; each row's symbiant name matched to its slot programmatically.
- **statsAndReqs:** QL, keyBonuses and reqs extracted from items.ocp (OMNICELL-CONTENT v3) with tools/mp-symbiant-extractor; stat numbers mapped from AOSharp.Common Stat.cs; ToWear = equip reqs, funcType 53045 (target=wearer) = stat bonuses.
- **professionLock:** Control unit ToWear stat-60 EqualTo {3,7,8,12} => Engineer/Trader/Bureaucrat/Engineer.
- **farmLocations:** Acquisition data is joined onto every item by AO item id from two sources: (1) the TinkerTools/TinkerPocket community drop database via its public API (https://ao.tinkeringidiot.com/tinkerpocket/), which supplies the pocket-boss / static-boss drop tables, and (2) hand-curated entries in tools/mp-symbiant-extractor/farm-sources-manual.json for the Legacy-of-the-Xan Betas, the crafted Alphas, the daily-reward/cash-shop lines and the one-off symbiants, each with its own guide URL. Regenerate with fetch-farm-sources.py then build-profile.py.
- **noDropFlags:** NODROP/Unique are read from the client data, not from a website: items.ocp Stats entry 0 (Flags), bit 26 = NoDrop and bit 27 = Unique (OmniCell.Enums/ItemFlags.cs). This SETTLED a source conflict: aoguide.org per-item flags were reported as marking 7 of the 13 Xan Betas NODROP, but the client data says only the Ear and Thigh Betas (the two quest rewards) are NODROP - every other Beta is tradeable. All 13 Alphas are NODROP, the Syndicate Brain symbiants are NODROP+Unique, the Prototype and Fabricated lines are NODROP, and the whole Aban line is tradeable.
- **crossCheck:** The per-slot Xan Beta -> boss mapping was produced twice, independently: from the ao-universe Legacy-of-the-Xan encounter guides and from the TinkerTools drop database. They agree on 11 of 13 slots; they differ only on Ear and Thigh (see _unverified).
- **tierLadder:** The 34-step Aban tier ladder (Unconscious QL1 ... Intelligent QL300) in `catalog` is read straight from items.ocp - adjective, QL, level requirement and Treatment requirement per tier - and matches, step for step, the ladder an independent dump of the TinkerTools symbiant table produced. Only QL1/QL5 and QL300 exist for all 13 slots; every intermediate tier exists for a subset of slots, which is why one pocket boss only ever yields certain slots.
- **expansionGate:** Expansion requirements are read from ToWear stat 389 BitAnd: the Aban/Prototype/Fabricated lines require bit 2 (Shadowlands) and the whole Xan line (Beta and Alpha) requires bit 128 (LegacyOfTheXan), per AOSharp CharacterFlags.cs ExpansionFlags.
- **professionParameterisation:** Regenerated for profession 3 (Engineer) with the same three-stage pipeline described in CLASS-PROFILE-PLAYBOOK.md 9a: mp-symbiant-extractor --prof 3 --slug engineer, then the shared profession-independent farm-sources.json, then build-profile.py --line Control. The --line value was PICKED FROM THE DATA (professionLockNames), not from memory: Engineer's only lock-set membership is Control. The 13 best-in-slot Alphas, the crafting recipe and the farm locations are item-level facts about the Control line, so they are identical to the Meta-Physicist's - the same item ids, verified again against itemnames.sql for this build.
