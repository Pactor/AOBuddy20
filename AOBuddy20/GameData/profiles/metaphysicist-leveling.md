# Meta-Physicist Leveling Path (1-220 + AI + Research)

Profession 12 - pet class. Companion to `metaphysicist-leveling.json`. For AOBuddy10.

The MP levels by **pet-tanking**: send the Attack pet, keep the Heal pet up, self-heal from
range. The pet takes the aggro and the hits; you stay safe and out-level content you could
never melee. Pets are persistent (no timer since 18.7) and can be cast **over-equipped** via a
Wrangle / Moch's buff to run above your own nano skills. Keep **Matter Creation (MC)** and
**Time & Space (TS)** maxed at all times - they gate pet quality every level.

> Sourcing: this is aggregated from AO-Universe, AODB wiki, the Funcom-forums MP guide, the
> Athen Paladins 1-220 progress guide, and the AO Fandom KB. See **Sources** and **Unverified**
> at the bottom. The sibling `metaphysicist-pets.json` / `metaphysicist-nanos.json` are the
> authoritative pet-nano ladder; this file is the *route*, not the nano list.

## The path in one paragraph

Newbie yard/Arete (1-6, free gear) -> Condemned Subway (6-25, loot the Abmouth Ring of the
Nucleus Basalis) -> Temple of Three Winds (25-60, do **all** boss loot before the hard level-60
entry lock) -> from 60 up chase **heckler teams** and the Shadowlands garden highway
(Nascence -> Elysium -> Scheol -> Adonis -> Penumbra -> Inferno), which is the fastest SK in
the game and where an MP is a wanted teammate (off-heals + pet DPS). Solo the whole way by
pet-tanking camps and Dark Ruins / Inferno missions. Run **daily quests** (RK city, Battle
Station, alien/mothership, LE dailies) in parallel for tokens, VP, AXP and XP once you own the
expansions, and grind **AI levels** in city defense where the pet tanks alien attackers safely.
Endgame is Inferno + APF/raids to 220 with QL240+ symbiants and near-complete research.

## Must-do quests & keys

- **Subway / Abmouth Supremus** (ICC HQ, Andromeda; instanced - team before entering): loot the
  **Ring of the Nucleus Basalis** (upgradeable NCU), Belt of Justice, Juggler's Treat.
- **TOTW access** via **Windcaller Karrec** (ICC HQ): Bronto Burger + Maddy Cardile credit card,
  then the portal. (Old Rome Green NPC Cerrak removed after 18.7.)
- **TOTW boss loot BEFORE level 60** (hard entry lock): Aztur the Immortal (Stygian Desolator),
  Uklesh the Frozen (Purifying Rod), the Curator (Notum Rings of Three), Lien (NCU memory),
  Guardian of Tomorrow (Guardian Tank Armor), robe tiers.
- **Aban Garden Key** (Nascence): collect ~3+ **Insignias of Aban** (Barking Chimeras / Unredeemed
  mobs) -> free garden hub travel.
- **Cama Garden Key** (Penumbra): 6 **Insignia of Vanya** (+ ~35 Ravine fragments) -> Penumbra
  hub travel/progression.
- **Per-zone garden/Sanctuary keys** (Nascence/Elysium/Scheol/Adonis/Penumbra): safe rez + travel.
- **Specialization quests** (Spec 1 ~80-110, Spec 2 ~150, Spec 3 Adonis group ~160+, Spec 4
  endgame): major power spikes.
- **Endgame chains**: Dust Brigade (register first), Arid Rift, Neretva Canyon.
- **Token board**: bravery + mission tokens; use Veteran Points for a cheap Yalmaha and token
  top-ups.

## Froob vs paid

- **Froob** (free = Classic RK + free Notum Wars): **hard cap level 200**, no SL, no AI (so
  **zero AI levels/perks/AXP**), no LE (so **no research**). Entire path is Rubi-Ka: yard ->
  Subway -> TOTW -> RK missions/Foreman's/Steps/Smuggler's Den/borg camps/The Reck to 200.
- **SL**: unlocks the heckler/garden leveling highway (fastest SK), garden keys, Sanctuary, and
  levels 201-220.
- **AI**: unlocks the parallel AXP / AI-level / perk track (city, mothership, sectors, dailies).
- **LE**: unlocks Personal + Global Research (skill/proc bonuses, mechs/turrets) and LE dailies.
- For an MP, **SL+AI+LE** together roughly double effective power (perks + research + better
  pets + safe heckler grind); froobs are fully viable but slower and cap at 200.

## MP-specific leveling tips

- **Send pet, stay back, heal it** - the whole class identity; lets you fight above your survivability.
- **Wrangle your pets**: Trader/General Wrangle or Moch's/Mocham's temporarily raises MC/TS so you
  can cast a pet above level; it persists after the buff (re-cast only when it dies) - carry a buff
  source. Watch `/oe` overequip.
- **Mezz pet = single-target STUN**, not an AoE calm; lock one add while the Attack pet kills
  another. Plan **single pet-pulls**; MP CC is weaker than a Crat's calms.
- **Max MC + TS above everything** (implants/symbiants + every Teachings/Composite/Mocham buff).
- **Loot the Abmouth Ring early** for cheap NCU so you can run more pet buffs at once.
- **Chase heckler teams from 60** - MP is wanted (off-heals + pet DPS) and heckler SK is the fastest.
- **Stack dailies** (city/BS/alien/LE) once you own the expansions for parallel tokens/VP/AXP/XP.
- **Grind AI levels in city defense** - pet tanks alien attackers while you heal = low-risk AXP.
- **Do garden/Sanctuary keys on zone entry** for safe rez points (matters for a squishy caster).
- **Nanomage** is the popular MP breed (Int/Psychic -> better pets) - relevant only at creation.
