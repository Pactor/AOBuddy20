# Nano-Technician — Gear Reference

Profession ID **11**, slug `nanotechnician`.

> **Every number in this file was read out of `items.ocp`** with
> `tools/eng-gear-extractor --prof 11 --slug nanotechnician --wield`
> (OMNICELL-CONTENT v3, client 18.8.50_EP1; 120,842 templates, 68,637 wearable,
> **61,530 Nano-Technician-usable** after the profession-lock rule). `nanotechnician-gear.json`
> is **generated by a script** from that dump — no stat figure in it is typed by hand — and the
> generator refuses to emit any row whose dump name disagrees with `itemnames.sql`.
> **488 / 488 id→name pairs verified, zero mismatches.**
> Implants, symbiants, nanos and weapons live in their own files and are not duplicated here.

## What a Nano-Technician stacks, in order

1. **Nano Damage Multiplier** (stat `0x218`) — a flat percentage on every nuke and DoT the class
   exists to cast. **Only 40 item names in the entire NT-usable dump grant it** (88 templates).
   This is the NT's Heal Multiplier: scarce, class-defining, and the thing to plan a set around.
2. **The six nano schools** — Matter Creation first (the nuke school), then the other five, because
   the NT's whole nano list is gated on them.
3. **Nano Point Cost** (`0x13E`) and **Max Nano** — a nuker's fight timer.
4. **Nano C. Init.** (`0x95`) — cast and recharge speed, i.e. damage per minute.
5. **Max NCU** — the NT runs a deep standing-buff stack.
6. **Max Health / Add. All Def. / evades** — it has the thinnest health pool of any caster and
   36 taunt/aggro nanos to make that worse.

## The Cyberdeck: the item that defines the class

`items.ocp` holds **97 Cyberdeck templates across 11 names**, every one locked to
`Profession EqualTo 11` and gated on **Shadowlands** (`Expansion BitAnd` bit 2). They occupy the
**right hand**. Every deck that grants anything also sets

> Pistol, Rifle, Assault Rifle, Shotgun, MG/SMG, Ranged Energy, Bow, Grenade, Heavy Weapons,
> Burst, Fling Shot, Aimed Shot and Full Auto to **−2000**

which does not "penalise" those skills — it makes **every ranged weapon in the game unusable**
while the deck is worn. A deck NT is a pure caster with an empty weapon slot. This is the single
biggest structural difference between this file and every other class profile written so far.

Chip molds are **QL 200+, NODROP, dropped by Alien bosses** in AI city raids; a **Disassembler
Tool** (Alien generals) destroys an applied mold so a different one can be fitted; **no tradeskill
skill is required**. ao-universe's figures for the three molds (+150 NanoC. Init., +3 % nano damage,
−5 % nano cost) match this client data **exactly**, which is why they are recorded as fact.

**One name, up to 14 ids.** Each deck name exists once per **breed + sex** combination — Solitus
and Nanomage share a template, Opifex has its own, Atrox has its own, each male and female. Playbook
§9b's "one name over many items" trap is the *norm* here, so every deck row in the JSON carries
`templatesWithThisName` + `allIds` and is read at a named id.

and **level 215**, and it is a key as much as a stat stick (the Temple of Home entrance accepts a
worn Base/Activated/Stellar Profession Nanodeck).

## Armor

### Combined Officer's (Alien Invasion) — **the pick, and the data says so without argument**
Six pieces (246670 / 246672 / 246674 / 246676 / 246678 / 246680), **identical at QL300**:
+30 to **all six** nano schools, +30 to eight trade skills, **+15 Max NCU, +200 Max Nano,
+50 Nano Pool, −3 % nano cost** each. Six pieces = **+180 every school, +90 Max NCU, +1200 Max
Nano, +300 Nano Pool, −18 % nano cost**. Requires Int 1000, Psychic 1000, Title Level 6.
Tradeskilled from **Arithmetic + Spiritual armour**, components from alien city raids.
Nothing else in the dump is close on nano skill.

All six Combined lines were compared piece-by-piece from the data before picking:
**Scout's** gives the same 14 skills and +90 NCU but swaps the nano block for +240 inits / +180
evades; **Paramedic's** gives *no nano skill at all* (+1200 HP, +300 Body Dev., +1200 nano,
−18 % cost) and is the survivability answer; **Commando's / Mercenary's / Sharpshooter's** are
pure weapon sets and give an NT nothing. All five are in the JSON under `alternativeAlienSets`.

### Ofab Nano Technician (Lost Eden, Victory Points) — **the only armour that carries NDM**
Three tiers, all `Profession = NanoTechnician`: base → **Improved** → **Penultimate**.
Body piece: **+2 % / +3 % / +4 % Nano Damage Multiplier** (264414 / 264412 / 264410) plus
750/850/1050 Max Nano, 30/35/40 Max NCU and −4/−5/−6 % nano cost. Penultimate QL300 requires
Int 1045, Psychic 845, Lost Eden.
Bought at the **profession armour terminal in the Unicorn Defense HUD** for **Victory Points**
(Battlestations / Alien Missions / Barkeeper missions) and upgraded twice with **Kyr'Ozch
Bio-Material — TYPE 468 for a Nano-Technician** (shared with Bureaucrat and Trader). Update QL must
be ≥ 80 % of the armour QL; *"Skills: none"*. The base version is tradeable and the upgrades are
NODROP — which the client data confirms (`noDrop=false` base, `true` for Improved/Penultimate).
Two extra slots exist outside the six-piece set: **OFAB Nano Technician Protective Gear** (267938,
back, **+2 % NDM**, +50 MM, +25 NCU) and **Shoulder Wear** (268081). There is also a
**Special Edition Ofab NT Helmet** (267384) that trades Matter Creation for −7 % nano cost,
+50 AAD and +500 Max Health.

**The practical endgame set is a mix:** Ofab body (for the Nano Damage Multiplier, which Combined
Officer's has none of) with Combined Officer's everywhere else (for the nano schools, which Ofab
barely touches).

### Everything else, ranked with its reason
- **Chosen (Clan) / Faithful (Omni) Nano Technician armor** — 8 slots including Back and Shoulders,
  −5 % nano cost and +32 NCU on the body. Verified **stat-identical** piece-for-piece except the
  cloak's Max Health (214807 = +400 vs 222747 = +150).
- **Notum Saturated Metaplast** (163334-163351) — NT-locked, **tradeable**, small but **even**
  bonuses to all six schools and +15 Nano C. Init. on every piece. The realistic twink set.
- **Miy's Nano Armor** — *demoted* for the NT. It spreads **one school per piece**, which is exactly
  wrong for a class that casts out of all six. (`doctor-gear.json` reaches the opposite conclusion
  for the opposite reason — a Doctor only needs BM and MM.)
- **Second Tier / First Tier Nano Technician** — the NT-locked ladder below Chosen/Faithful. First
  Tier gives +300 Max Nano and nothing else worth a slot.
- **Azure Reveries** body piece (165304) and **Loren's** upgrade (304613) — −10 % nano cost,
  no expansion and no profession requirement.

## The trap this pass caught: items an NT can never have

A criteria-only "what can profession 11 equip" sweep reports **23 templates (20 item names)** as NT
gear that an NT can never obtain. They are **Meta-Physicist Creations** — conjured by the MP's
`Creation: <item>` nanos (FunctionType 53064 `SpawnItem`), NODROP, and four of them additionally
gate on **`NanoFocusLevel` (0x163) BitAnd 64**, a state **no item in the entire 120,842-template
pack grants**. `nanotechnician-nanos.json` contains **zero** SpawnItem nanos, so an NT cannot create
one.

Left unfiltered, **Shield of Esa (275851)** would have been printed as the NT's single best item in
the game at **+25 % Nano Damage Multiplier** — two and a half times the best real one. Also caught:
Shield of Zset (273377/305605, +500 Nano C. Init.), Asp of Titaniush (275848), Mocham's Guard
(154927, −10 % nano cost), Wave Breaker, Wixel's Notum Python, Azure Cobra of Orma, Asp of Semol,
Gold Acantophis. All are excluded from every ranking and listed with their reason under
`notObtainableByNanoTechnician`, with the exclusion list built **at generation time** from the
verified `metaphysicist-nanos.json`, never typed by hand.

`Aggressive Staff of Julian Redfire` (275019) is NanoFocus-gated but is *not* an MP creation; how the
gate is satisfied is **unknown**, so it is flagged rather than recommended.

## Caveats

- **150 templates** carry a `HairTexture BitAnd 65536` entry in their ToWear criteria (Combined
  Officer's Headwear among them). It is printed faithfully in the `reqs` string but is almost
  certainly the client's "this headwear hides hair" rendering flag, not a player requirement.
  Not verified either way.
- **`Blackmane's Stat Buffer` (206704)** grants +2,000,000 Max Health and +2000 to every skill. It
  is a developer/GM template and is deliberately excluded from every ranking — it tops a naive sort
  of the dump on Nano C. Init.
- Cyberdeck **credit prices** come from ao-universe's guide; prices are not in the client data.
- **No `nanotechnician-pets.json`.** The NT has no pets and `nanotechnician` is already in the
  renderer's `PETLESS` list in `tools/aodb/app.py`.
- `tools/aodb/app.py` gained **one additive change**: a `gear.extraTables` loop at the end of
  `renderGearTab()`, which renders the Nano Damage Multiplier list, the Cyberdeck ladder, the
  Nanodeck chain, the nano-cost / nano-init tables and the profession-locked catalog. It renders
  only when a profile supplies the key, so no other class's gear tab changes.
  **The server must be restarted for it to take effect.**
