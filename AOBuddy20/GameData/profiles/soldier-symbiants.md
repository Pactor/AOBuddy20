# Soldier - Symbiants: best-in-slot AND where to farm them

A top Soldier fills all 13 implant slots with ARTILLERY-Unit symbiants - the ranged-weapon unit. The Artillery line is profession-locked to Soldier, Fixer, Agent, Adventurer and Trader (ToWear stat-60 EqualTo {1,4,5,6,7}, verified in items.ocp; 293 of the 305 Artillery templates a Soldier can wear carry that lock). Summed over the complete 13-slot Xan Artillery ALPHA set, read from items.ocp, the line gives +289 to EVERY ranged weapon skill a Soldier uses (Assault Rifle, Shotgun, MG/SMG, Ranged Energy, Heavy Weapons, Pistol, Rifle, Grenade, Sharp Object, Multi Ranged), +289 to every ranged special (Burst, Full Auto, Fling Shot, Aimed Shot), +289 Ranged Init, +289 Evade Close / Dodge Ranged / Duck Exp, +1149 Max Health and +180 Add. All Def. That is the whole Soldier stat sheet in one line, which is why symbiants beat cluster implants for this profession outright. Best-in-slot at endgame is the Xan Artillery-Unit ALPHA in every slot; the Xan Artillery BETA is the lower-requirement stepping stone (Treatment 1870 / level 201 against 2088 / 219). WHERE TO GET THEM: the Artillery ladder is Shadowlands pocket-boss loot (summon the boss at an Incarnator from a crafted Novictalized Notum Crystal) up to QL280; the QL300 'Intelligent ... Artillery Unit Aban' for every slot comes from The Night Heart on Pandemonium Antenora; the Xan Betas come from the three Legacy-of-the-Xan Central Hub encounters except Ear and Thigh, which are quest rewards; the Alphas are crafted, never looted. See farmingRuns for the location-by-location plan and acquisitionBySlot for the per-slot ladder.

**Farm coverage:** acquisition verified for **244 of 305** Artillery-unit symbiants usable by a Soldier; **61** are left `unknown` rather than guessed.

## Which symbiant line?

- **Recommended unit: Artillery** - Artillery is the ranged-weapon unit. Verified from the client data, not from memory: every Artillery symbiant's ToWear action profession-locks to stat 60 EqualTo {1 Soldier, 4 Fixer, 5 Agent, 6 Adventurer, 7 Trader} (items.ocp), and the extractor run with --prof 1 returns 293 Artillery templates against 0 from Control / Extermination / Infantry / Support.

## Farming methods

**pocketBoss:** Pocket boss: farm the four pattern pieces (Aban/Abhan, Bhotaar, Chi, Dom) from the listed mobs, combine them in order into a Complete Blueprint Pattern, combine that with a Crystal Filled by the Source (Nano Programming 6x QL), novictalize it with Subdued Flow of Novictum via an Ancient Novictum Refiner (Quantum FT 4.5x QL), then right-click the crystal on an Incarnator whose QL is >= the boss QL. Incarnator QL caps: Nascense 75 | Elysium South 115 / North 130 | Scheol South 140 / North 160 | Adonis City East 170 / West 190 | Penumbra West (The Pipe) 200 / East (Fortress) 215 | Inferno South (Barracks) 225 / North (Frontier) 255 | Pandemonium 270.

## Alpha crafting

**what:** Xan Alpha symbiants are the most advanced symbiants in AO. They cannot be looted - each is crafted by the player from two QL300 components, is NODROP (items.ocp Flags bit 26) and cannot be reverted once assembled.

**recipe:** Combine (drag + shift-click, or the tradeskill window) a Xan <slot> Symbiant, Artillery Unit Beta with an Intelligent <slot> Symbiant, Artillery Unit Aban QL300 of the SAME slot and SAME unit line. No tradeskill tool or nano is required.

**components:**
- xanBeta: OrderedDict({'what': 'Xan Artillery-Unit Beta for the slot, QL300, Treatment 1870 / level 201 to wear.', 'bySource': OrderedDict({'Technomaster Sinuh (final boss of Mitaar Hero) -- Central Hub (Xan Hub), Alien Outzones': OrderedDict({'how': 'raid/instance drop', 'slots': ['Head/Brain', 'Chest/Body', 'Left Wrist', 'Left Hand']}), 'Ground Chief Vortexx -- Central Hub (Xan Hub), Alien Outzones': OrderedDict({'how': 'raid/instance drop', 'slots': ['Eye/Ocular', 'Left Arm', 'Right Wrist', 'Waist']}), "Kyr'Ozch Envoy is the final kill; the symbiant is handed over on completion -- Arid Rift (entered from Central Gateway / Central Hub)": OrderedDict({'how': 'quest reward (NOT a drop)', 'slots': ['Ear']}), 'Deranged Xan -- Central Hub (Xan Hub), Alien Outzones': OrderedDict({'how': '12-man raid drop', 'slots': ['Right Arm', 'Right Hand', 'Feet']}), 'handed over by Lance Corporal DeGeus on completing Area X -- Neretva Canyons': OrderedDict({'how': 'quest reward (NOT a drop)', 'slots': ['Legs/Thigh']})})})
- intelligentQL300: Intelligent Artillery-Unit (Aban) QL300 for the slot - the top-QL Shadowlands symbiant. For all 13 Artillery slots the only recorded source is The Night Heart on Pandemonium Antenora (level-250 static raid boss, NOT a pocket boss - it cannot be summoned at an Incarnator). Tradeable (no NoDrop bit in items.ocp), so it can also be bought.

**equipRequirements:**
- note: Requirements to WEAR the finished Artillery Alpha, read from items.ocp ToWear. Identical across all 13 slots except the one variable ability.
- Treatment: 2088
- level: 219
- fixedAbilities: Artillery's two fixed abilities are Agility and Strength, each 1276. The Meta-Physicist's Control line uses Intelligence + Psychic instead - this is the concrete place the Soldier twink target differs.
- variableAbilityBySlot: The third equip ability varies by slot (Intelligence, Sense, Stamina or Strength), each 1276 - see each bestInSlot row's reqs.

**clickyTrick:** Xan Beta Ear and Thigh are the quest rewards and the only two that are clickable: right-click cycles their unit type through all five lines. The other eleven Betas are NOT clickable - their unit is fixed on drop. Money-saver: buy the cheapest Intelligent Ear/Thigh of any unit, cycle the Beta to match, combine, then cycle the resulting Alpha back to Artillery.

**upgradeOverBase:** Per slot the Alpha beats both components. Brain example, all three figures read from items.ocp: the Beta gives Ranged Energy / Bow Spc Att / Treatment / Comp. Lit. +130 and Ranged Init +79; the Intelligent QL300 gives +137 / +83; the Alpha gives +144 / +87 and adds Psychology, Map Navigation and the three Vehicle skills.

## Verification

- **namesAndIds:** All 39 ids (13 Intelligent QL300 + 13 Xan Beta + 13 Xan Alpha) come from items.ocp and were name-verified against itemnames.sql by the extractor; each row's symbiant name was matched to its slot programmatically, not by hand.
- **statsAndReqs:** QL, keyBonuses and reqs extracted from items.ocp (OMNICELL-CONTENT v3) with tools/mp-symbiant-extractor; stat numbers mapped from AOSharp.Common Stat.cs; ToWear = equip reqs, funcType 53045 (target=wearer) = stat bonuses.
- **keyStatSet:** keyBonuses for this profession are rendered with the WEAPON key set (build-profile.py --key-stats weapon), added in the Soldier pass. The Meta-Physicist's caster key set silently dropped Assault Rifle, Shotgun, MG/SMG, Ranged Energy, Heavy Weapons, Burst, Full Auto, Fling Shot, Aimed Shot and Ranged Init from every Artillery row - that is, the entire point of the line.
- **professionLock:** Artillery unit ToWear stat-60 EqualTo {1,4,5,6,7} => Soldier / Fixer / Agent / Adventurer / Trader. Counts from the --prof 1 extractor run: Artillery 293, unlocked 14, Soldier-only 1, all other unit lines 0.
- **farmLocations:** Acquisition is joined onto every item by AO item id from (1) the TinkerTools/TinkerPocket community drop database via its public API and (2) hand-curated, guide-cited entries in tools/mp-symbiant-extractor/farm-sources-manual.json. The farm data is profession-independent, so it is the same file the Meta-Physicist pass validated. Regenerate with fetch-farm-sources.py then build-profile.py.
- **noDropFlags:** NODROP/Unique are read from items.ocp Stats entry 0 (Flags), bit 26 = NoDrop, bit 27 = Unique (OmniCell.Enums/ItemFlags.cs) - not from a website. All 13 Artillery Alphas are NODROP; the Ear and Thigh Betas (the quest rewards) are NODROP; every other Beta and the whole Aban line are tradeable.
- **setTotals:** The per-skill set totals in `summary` and unitChoice.setTotalsAcrossAll13XanAlphas are SUMMED by the generator from the 13 Alpha rows' own bonus tables in items.ocp, not quoted from a guide.
