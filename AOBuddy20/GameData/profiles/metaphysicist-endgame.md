# Meta-Physicist (Profession 12) — Endgame, Raids & Gear Progression

Research profile for AOBuddy10. Anarchy Online level cap is **220** plus up to ~30 AI/research levels.
"Endgame" here means roughly **level 200–220, AI 24–30, research L9–10**. Sourced to reputable AO
references (ao-universe.com, wiki.aodb.us, forums.funcom.com, athenpaladins.org, fandom KB).
Anything I could not fully pin down is flagged in **Unverified** at the bottom.

Key fixed fact: **Meta-Physicist uses the CONTROL symbiant unit type** (shared with Engineer,
Bureaucrat, Trader). Match "Control" on all Beta/Alpha/Intelligent symbiants.

---

## 1. Endgame content & raids

### Shadowlands pinnacle
- **Pandemonium (Adonis inner realm)** — raid, ~level 201–220, needs a **+10 (or better) Pande access ring**
  to reach the inner islands. 18 bosses across 4 islands (4 portal bosses gate progress and drop nothing;
  12 "phatz" bosses drop tradeable phatz used game-wide). **For MP:** the **Night Heart** drops
  **Intelligent (QL300) symbiants** — the second half of every Xan Alpha. **Role:** pet DPS, root/calm on
  adds, nukes; keep the attack pet alive and re-summon fast.
- **The Beast** — the classic SL pinnacle kill. Per kill: 4 armor parts + both hilts + 1–2 stars + 2–4
  weapons (each as **Lady of** QL299 or **Lord of** QL300) + **1 random Shadowbreed**. MP-usable star
  reported: **Star of Moral** (~1.9%). **Role:** filler pet DPS + support; not a core Beast role.

### Legacy of the Xan (LoX)
- **The Xan (12-man) / Hall of the Elders** — endgame raid, level ~210+, **4-hour lockout**, in the Xan
  Center Hub. **For MP:** drops **Xan Beta symbiants** (Control-line) — the first half of every Alpha —
  plus Xan spirits, weapon/belt tuning & upgrade devices, and Nanodeck components. Completion gives
  **The Awakening** (5h buff, level 210+) and an Offense/Defense buff from the Deranged Xan. **Role:** pet
  DPS + nukes; note MPs are explicitly targeted by the encounter's **mind-control** mechanic — manage/re-summon
  pets through control phases.

### Alien Invasion (AI)
- **Alien City raids (Kyr'Ozch cities)** — high-level raid. City generals + alien-ship bosses drop
  **Kyr'Ozch Bio-Material** (upgrades alien weapons, crafts alien armor). Bio types: **Pristine** (least
  chem), **Mutated** (most chem), **Viralbots** (Spiritual Lead Viralbots for the caster armor). This is the
  material farm for Miy's/Combined caster armor and weapon upgrades.
- **Sector 7** — high-level team content; gates open ~every 7h for ~10 min (via Unicorn Outpost, ship
  outside ICC/Andromeda). 4 ship bosses (4th spawned from a Power Core from the first three). Drops **16
  NODROP profession-restricted Special Edition Kyr'Ozch weapons** and QL160–270 **VP** items — check for
  MP-usable SE weapons.
- **Sectors 10 / 13 / 28 / 35** — mid→high-level instanced alien raids: alien loot, bio-material, VP.
  Difficulty scales up toward S35.
- **Sector 42 (Alien Playfield / APF) — the Zodiac / Artillery Commander** — endgame raid, level 210+.
  Requires **3 bosses in separate locations killed simultaneously** to spawn the **Artillery Commander**
  (biggest alien in the game). Drops **APF nano items** and endgame alien loot. *Don't roll on 210+ APF
  items until you are level 210.*
- **Battlestation (Ofab / Victory Points)** — faction battlefield; primary purpose is farming **Victory
  Points**, spent at the OFab Shop for **Ofab armor** (incl. the **Ofab Metaphysicist** set, each piece
  upgradable twice), Ofab weapons, nanos, and the QL300 ring / SE Head / Ofab back at 220.

### Shadowlands progression content
- **Penumbra missions** — level 150–210 team range; rolled missions give **Shadowbreed gear** + tokens
  (~205–210 is the sweet spot). MP clears these easily with pets.
- **Inferno missions & pocket bosses** — level 190+; grind **tokens**, Inferno boots/HHAB items, and the
  **9 QL220–255 pocket-boss patterns** needed for the Galahad Garden key. Highly soloable for MP.
- **Galahad Garden Key quest** — opens the Inferno garden (safe staging, buffs, no DoT) — important for
  staging Pande/Inferno raids. Requires the 9 patterns above.
- **Ice Golem questline** — Inferno progression: give Cama the heart → Ice Golem blueprint → spawn at the
  Incarnator → drops 6 molars.

### Dailies / repeatables
- **Lost Eden dailies** (incl. the **Arid AI-team daily** that yields warp-device parts) and **team dailies**
  like *Go to Sector 07 (Team)* / Elysium dailies — steady **tokens, VP, AI/research XP**. MP clears fast.

### Dust Brigade chain
- **DB1 Outpost → DB2 Inside the Machine → DB3 The Facility** — high-level → endgame instances, full team
  (tank + doc). **Enhanced Dust Brigade Armor** drops in DB1/DB2 (caster-viable). DB3 is six floors (1st/3rd/5th
  are filler). Completing the DB quest is a **215–220 progression milestone**.

## 2. Gear / progression targets (in order)

1. **Endgame nano twink (foundation)** — as **Nanomage**, push **Matter Creation (MC)** and **Time & Space
   (TS)** high enough to cast the top pets (older guide cites ~**MC 3045 / TS 3040** for ~L225 pets — see
   Unverified). Always run the best attack/heal/mezz pet you can cast. Everything else exists partly to raise
   MC/TS/nano-init so you can hold bigger pets.
2. **Base implants → first symbiants** — QL200+ implants, then swap in **Control** symbiants toward a stable
   **QL240+** baseline (a 220 checkbox).
3. **Ofab armor via Battlestation VP** — buy the **Ofab Metaphysicist** set; upgrade each piece twice. Grab
   Penumbra Shadow gear along the way. Ofab replaced Tier as the baseline profession armor.
4. **Alien caster armor (Miy's Nano / Combined)** — farm bio-material + Spiritual Lead Viralbots; **Miy's
   Nano** gives Int/Psychic + skills (biggest on **Head + Arms**). Alien **Spiritual** = nano pool,
   **Arithmetic** = nano + trade skills. Best caster armor in the game.
5. **Research + AI/perks** — Personal + Global research to **L9–10**, AI to **24–30**, complete perk lines.
   Personal research caps at **5% of total cost per reward**, so bank big XP/SK rewards. Large permanent
   power, no drop RNG.
6. **Jewelry / rings + VP purchases** — a **+10 Pande access ring** (gates Intelligent QL300 farming); at 220
   buy **QL300 ring + Special Edition Head + Ofab back** with VP (~**84k VP** total). Grab caster-usable
   Beast stars/rings.
7. **Xan Beta symbiants** — raid **Hall of the Elders / Xan 12-man** for Control Betas (ear/thigh are
   adaptable between unit types). Only source.
8. **Intelligent QL300 symbiants** — farm the **Night Heart in Pandemonium** (few per kill = the bottleneck;
   needs the +10 ring).
9. **Xan Alpha symbiants (craft, NODROP)** — combine matching **Beta + Intelligent QL300** (same unit type +
   slot) **on your own MP**. Best-in-slot symbiants. Use the ear/thigh right-click adapt trick to save money.
10. **Dust Brigade armor + questlines** — DB1/DB2 for Enhanced DB armor, finish DB3 + Arid Rift / Neretva;
    round out with Beast Lord/Lady weapons and Shadowbreed. Long, save-for-last chores.

## 3. MP-specific endgame notes

- **Three pets, always up:** attack (DPS), heal (sustain), mezz (crowd control). Bring the right pet for the
  fight and cast the best you can. This is the MP's whole identity endgame.
- **Raid role:** flexible **utility/DPS**, not a required slot (unlike tank/doc). Value = pet DPS + roots/calms
  on adds + nukes + nano-skill debuffs on bosses.
- **Pet survivability is the core problem:** AoE and boss specials delete pets. Keep the heal pet up, position
  the attack pet, re-summon fast, and budget nano for constant re-casts. Expect to lose pets in Beast/Xan/APF
  and rebuild.
- **Breed:** Nanomage is standard for max Int/Psychic and nano pool (biggest castable pets/nanos).
- **Twinking for endgame nano:** the whole gear ladder (symbiants → Ofab → Miy's/Combined → jewelry →
  research) is chosen to push MC/TS/nano-init and nano pool so you can cast and sustain the top pets.
- **NODROP / self-craft:** Xan Alpha symbiants and DB armor are made/earned on the MP that wears them — don't
  plan to buy finished Alphas.
