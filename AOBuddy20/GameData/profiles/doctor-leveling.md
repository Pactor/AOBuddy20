# Doctor — Leveling 1-220 (+ AI / research)

Profession ID **10**.

> Level bands are taken **verbatim** from the AODB `Hunting_Grounds` table (one source, so they are traceable). Every nano threshold and item id in the per-band commentary is read from `doctor-nanos.json` and `items.ocp`. Carried-over material is marked in the flagged section.

## The one strategy

**Always be in a team.** A Doctor is the only profession that gets an instant invite at every level from 8 to 220, and team SK on a heckler run dwarfs anything a Doctor can do alone. Solo is the fallback, not the plan.

**The solo loop, when you must:** land the best initiative debuff you can cast → stack **one DoT from each of the three strains** (`DOT_LineA`, `DOT_LineB`, `DOTStrainC` are separate nanolines, so all three tick at once) → auto-attack with a pistol → heal through it. You will outlive anything at your level; you just kill slowly.

## Key quests and gates

- **Subway / Abmouth** (8-25) → Ring of the Nucleus Basalis, your first NCU fix.
- **TOTW access** (Windcaller Karrec at ICC HQ: Bronto Burger + Maddy Cardile credit card) → **entry hard-locks past level 60**.
- **Doctor Suit chain** (60-205): First Tier 217698-217707 → Second Tier 214755-214768 → Chosen 214841-214858 *or* Faithful 217710-217725.
- **Specialization 1-4** — **not optional flavour for a Doctor**: every level-gated SL single heal (223281-223299) carries a `Specialization BitAnd 8` cast requirement, so the spec chain is a prerequisite for the top of your own heal ladder.
- Garden keys: **Aban** (Nascence), Elysium, **Cama** (Penumbra), **Galahad** (Inferno, 9 pocket-boss patterns).
- **Pandemonium access ring (+10 or better)** — gates the Intelligent QL300 symbiants and therefore the Alphas.

## Froob vs paid

A free account (cap 200, no SL/AI/LE) **loses**: Improved Instinctive Control (222856), Team-Enhanced Deathless Blessing (269455), every level-gated SL single heal (223281-223299), Misdiagnosis (266306), everything gated on `NanoFocusLevel BitAnd 64` (270747, 273315, 273312, 275011, 275701), all research procs and all alien perks.

It **keeps**: Complete Healing, Alpha and Omega, 36 of 38 team heals, all 16 team HP buffs, all 14 short HP buffs, the full HoT ladder, all four heal-delta buffs, Iron Circle, Instinctive Control, Nano Repulsor, Superior First Aid, Epsilon Purge and **UBT**.

That is a far smaller loss than most professions take — **the Doctor is one of the strongest froob classes**, because its defining nanos are all pre-Shadowlands and Loren's Azure Reveries gives it a full no-expansion caster set.

## Tips

- Cast the **team HP buff before every pull** — it lasts ~1h and heals the health it adds.
- **Max Treatment every level** (cost 1.0), and buff your own with Superior First Aid (+80).
- **Don't skip Time & Space** — the 16-rung team HP buff line is the only Doctor line that needs it.
- **Don't skip Matter Creation** — short HP buffs, heal-delta buffs and all DoTs need it. AOWiki: *"Even a Doctor that wants only healing and buffs cannot forego Matter Creation"*.
- **Budget NCU**: Abmouth ring at 10, Accelerated NCU Memory chips (164603-164608), and the **Internship** research line raises NCU directly.
- **TOTW boss loot before 60.** No way back in.
- **Make friends with a Trader and an MP** — a wrangle plus Composite Mochams is often exactly one rung up the heal ladder.
- **Twink Cure for Baldness** (246274) if rerolling: Doctor-locked, gated only on Pharma Tech 499 (cost 1.0 for you), **no level requirement**, and gives +2 Heal Multiplier, +20/+20 Treatment/First Aid, +50 Nano Pool, +50 Body Dev and 1000 AC.
- **Keep the aggro when you get it.** The Funcom teaming guide: *"If the doctor takes aggro, let her keep it. She is the healer and it's easier for her to heal herself then to heal anyone else."*

## Where a Doctor differs from the MP template

- **No `petNotes` field** — the MP's tactical model is "send the pet, stay back"; the Doctor's is "get into a team; solo by debuffing and out-lasting". (The field is `classNotes`, which `tools/aodb/app.py` already renders.)
- Per-band content is keyed to **nano-skill thresholds read from castReqs**, not to pet tiers.
- The froob section reaches the **opposite** conclusion about severity — the Doctor loses little, because its defining nanos are all pre-SL.
- The Specialization entry carries a **hard mechanical consequence** the MP file has no equivalent of.
- Bands come from **one** table rather than a blend of guides, so they are traceable.
