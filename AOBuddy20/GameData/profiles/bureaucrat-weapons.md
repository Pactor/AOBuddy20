# Bureaucrat - Weapons

Bureaucrat weapons and allowed special attacks, for AOBuddy10 bot decision-making. Generated by `tools/mp-weapon-extractor/gen-weapons-profile.ps1 -ProfessionId 8 -Slug bureaucrat` from `items.ocp`; every id verified against `itemnames.sql`.

## How the weapon-skill list was chosen

The client data does not encode 'profession X uses weapon skill Y', so the list is operator-supplied - but each entry is justified by three measured signals recorded per type: (1) skillCostFactor/skillCostRank from reference/skillcaps.json, (2) weaponsProfessionLocked, the count of templates whose ToWield locks them to this profession, and (3) professionNanoBuffLines, the profession's own nano lines that buff that skill, read from bureaucrat-nanos.json.

**The selection rule applied here, stated so it can be argued with:** include a weapon skill if its Bureaucrat IP cost factor is 3.2 or lower, OR if at least one template in `items.ocp` is ToWield-locked to profession 8 in that skill. That admits the seven below. Pistol at **1.6** is the only genuinely cheap weapon skill a Bureaucrat has; the next is Martial Arts at 2.8, and everything not listed costs **4.0**.

## Coverage, honestly

- 9,684 of the 10,730 weapon templates in `items.ocp` are wieldable by profession 8 (no ToWield Profession(60) lock to another class).
- Across the seven chosen skills that is **3960 usable templates**, of which this file lists **68 representatives** (every Bureaucrat-locked model, plus the top models per weapon line by dpsProxy). The EXHAUSTIVE per-model catalogue is `bureaucrat-weapons-endgame.json` (474 endgame models); this file is the curated view on top of it.
- Where each representative comes from is in `bureaucrat-weapon-sources.json` - 43 of 68 ids carry a cited acquisition, 25 say `unknown`.

## Deviations from the template classes

- PISTOL IS THE ONLY CHEAP WEAPON SKILL A BUREAUCRAT HAS: IP cost factor 1.6. The next cheapest is Martial Arts at 2.8, then 1h Blunt / MG-SMG / Shotgun / Brawling / Sharp Objects at 3.2, and EVERYTHING else - including Assault Rifle, Rifle, Ranged Energy, Bow, Grenade, Heavy Weapons, both 2h skills, 1h Edged, Piercing, Melee Energy and every ranged special including Fling Shot - is 4.0. The Shade file could list three real options and the Soldier file eight; the Crat has one.
- The seven skills here were chosen by a rule that is stated rather than remembered: include a skill if its IP cost is 3.2 or lower, OR if at least one weapon in items.ocp is profession-locked to Bureaucrat in that skill. That admits Pistol (1.6), Martial Arts (2.8), MG-SMG, Shotgun and 1h Blunt (3.2), plus Ranged Energy and Melee Energy (both 4.0, but carrying Crat-locked models).
- 28 WEAPONS ARE LOCKED TO BUREAUCRAT - 24 Pistol, 3 Ranged Energy, 1 Melee Energy. The Metaphysicist file's equivalent block is named metaphysicistOnlyWeapons; this file uses the generic professionOnlyWeapons key. The Crat's locked list is almost pure Pistol, which is the single strongest piece of in-data evidence for the skill choice above.
- THE 'SMALL EBONY FIGURINE' TRAP BITES HERE, ON A CRAT-LOCKED ITEM. Playbook section 9b warns that one name covers nine templates. Two of them are Bureaucrat-locked and they are DIFFERENT WEAPONS: 202404 is QL223 Melee Energy (req 649) and 202420 is QL203 Pistol (req 549). Any row that printed 'Small Ebony Figurine' without its id would be wrong about the weapon skill.
- There is no Multi Melee or Multi Ranged weapon a Bureaucrat can wield: of the 6 MultiMelee templates in the data, 0 are usable by profession 8. Dual wield for a Crat is gated by the character's Multi skill (cost 4.0), not by the weapon.

## Unverified / caveats

- The client data has no 'profession X uses weapon skill Y' table. The seven skills in usableWeaponTypes are an OPERATOR CHOICE made on a stated, data-driven rule (see howWeaponSkillsWereChosen and the _deviations block); the justification numbers beside each are computed, the selection itself is a judgement.
- Wield requirements, specials and damage are read at each representative's own QL and scale with QL.
- 'Where to get' for these representatives lives in bureaucrat-weapon-sources.json; 25 of the 68 representative ids have no cited acquisition and say so.
