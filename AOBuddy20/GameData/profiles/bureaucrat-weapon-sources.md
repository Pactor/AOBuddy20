## Deviations from the template classes

- 63% cited coverage (43 of 68) on a set that is mostly Pistol. The Pistol lines a Crat actually wants - Xan (Lust of the Xan), Ofab, Kyr'Ozch, Dreadloch and the Crat-locked Motivator / Education / Revoked pistols - are the well-documented ones, so the covered fraction is concentrated exactly where a player looks, while the unknowns are mostly generic Rubi-Ka models.
- Unlike the Soldier file, no source here is a shop. Nothing in the Bureaucrat's endgame pistol list is vendor-bought on either allowed source, so 'shop' never appears as a how.

## Unverified

- 43 of the 68 representative ids carry a cited acquisition, joined by model name and id from bureaucrat-weapons-endgame.json, which cites ao-universe.com and wiki.aodb.us. 25 say 'unknown'.
- 'unknown' means no acquisition is documented on the two allowed sources. The per-item drop databases (auno.org, aoitems.com) are blocked to automation, so no drop mob or playfield is approximated - 6 of those 25 at least carry an expansion gate read from the item itself, which narrows where they can come from without naming it.
