## Best by weapon skill

The picks are **computed**, not a community tier list. The criteria, quoted from the file:

- **summary** - Picks are computed from the items' OWN stats in items.ocp plus this profession's skill cost factors. They are NOT a community tier list and are not presented as one.
- **dpsProxy** - avgDamage / (attackDelaySec + rechargeDelaySec), where avgDamage = (MinDamage+MaxDamage)/2 and the two delays are the item's AttackDelay (stat 294) and RechargeDelay (stat 210) in 1/100 s. This is a COMPARISON PROXY ONLY: it ignores initiative caps, crit rate, add-damage, damage modifiers, nano buffs and special attacks, so it ranks these weapons against each other on the item data - it is not in-game DPS.
- **ipWeightedReq** - Sum over every ToWield skill and special-attack requirement of (requiredValue x this profession's cost factor for that skill, from reference/skillcaps.json). Lower means cheaper for this profession to actually reach. It is a relative IP-burden number, not an IP total.
- **topDamage** - The model with the highest dpsProxy in the skill. Reported with its gates (expansion, title level) so an unreachable pick is obvious.
- **bestValue** - Among models that still reach at least 60% of that skill's top dpsProxy - i.e. among weapons that are actually competitive - the one with the highest dpsProxy / ipWeightedReq. The 60% floor is there because without it the metric just picks the weakest, cheapest weapon in the skill; it is a stated threshold, not a fact.
- **bestWithoutExpansion** - The highest dpsProxy model whose ToWield carries NO Expansion(389) gate - i.e. the best option for a character without the expansions. Null if every endgame model in that skill is expansion-gated.
- **gates** - expansion = the ToWield Expansion(389) BitAnd criterion, authoritative. titleLevelReq = the ToWield TitleLevel(37) criterion: 6 = level 190+, 7 = level 205+.
- **caveat** - Where two models tie on dpsProxy the one with the lower ipWeightedReq is listed first. 'Best' here means best on these stated measures and nothing more.

## Xan weapon acquisition walkthrough

The 'Xan' weapons are NOT a drop. Every one of them starts as a Shadowlands Beast weapon (or a Sword of Dawn/Dusk), which is then upgraded with a Xan Weapon Upgrade Device that drops in the Legacy of the Xan team instances. The combine needs no tradeskill skill.

**Expansions needed**

- Shadowlands - for Pandemonium, where the starting weapon drops.
- Legacy of the Xan booster - for the Central Hub instances the upgrade device drops in, AND to wield the finished weapon (the item's own Expansion(389) criterion carries bit 7 = LegacyOfTheXan).
- Alien Invasion - only for the optional High Lord of Angst side-step.

**Level band:** Content level 201-220. The finished 'X of the Xan' weapons require title level 6 (level 190+) in their own ToWield criteria; the 'Corrupted Lord of X' versions require title level 7 (level 205+).

### Step 1 - Get the starting piece: a Beast weapon.

Kill The Beast, the final boss of the Pandemonium playfield (Shadowlands). Pandemonium is reached by killing the portal bosses that open the blue portal onward; an instanced version exists via an altar just inside the zone. The Night Heart is the mid-boss. The Beast respawns on a 9-hour timer.

- **Drops:** The Beast drops the weapon as 'Lord of <Sin>' (QL 300) or 'Lady of <Sin>' (QL 299) - identical items otherwise. Over 104 documented kills The Beast dropped 2-4 weapons, 4 armour pieces, both hilts, 1-2 profession stars and one Shadowbreed per kill. No per-weapon drop rate is published.
- **Which one:** Pick the sin that matches the weapon skill you want. Read from the client data (each Lord/Lady template's own primary wield skill in items.ocp): Abandonment = 1h Blunt, Anger = Assault Rifle, Angst = Rifle, Chaos = Bow, Deceit = Piercing, Envy = MG/SMG, Gluttony = 2h Blunt, Greed = Shotgun, Hatred = 1h Edged, Lust = Pistol, Pride = 2h Edged, Sloth = Grenade, Wisdom = Martial Arts. For Bureaucrat the usable starting pieces are: Lord/Lady of Abandonment (1h Blunt) -> Abandonment of the Xan; Lord/Lady of Envy (MG/SMG) -> Envy of the Xan; Lord/Lady of Greed (Shotgun) -> Greed of the Xan; Lord/Lady of Lust (Pistol) -> Lust of the Xan; Lord/Lady of Wisdom (Martial Arts) -> Wisdom of the Xan. There is NO Lord/Lady of <sin> weapon, and therefore no Xan weapon, for these skills on this profession's list: Ranged Energy, Melee Energy.

- Source: https://www.ao-universe.com/guides/shadowlands/quests-guides-2/pandemonium/pandemonium-2

### Step 1b - Melee Energy only: build a Sword of Dawn or Sword of Dusk instead.

There is no 'Lord of' weapon for Melee Energy. Clan/Redeemed: Notum Seed + Sunrise Hilt = Sword of Dawn. Omni/Unredeemed: Novictum Seed + Sunset Hilt = Sword of Dusk. This step needs 500 Weapon Smithing - the only real skill requirement in the whole line.

- **Drops:** Notum Seed / Novictum Seed drop from The Night Heart in Pandemonium (Redeemed and Unredeemed side respectively); not 100%, and both also appear in player shops. Sunrise Hilt (Clan) and Sunset Hilt (Omni) are 100% NODROP drops from The Beast - recorded at 18.27% and 4.81% over 104 kills. Neutrals capped at 25,000 faction cannot get these.
- **Which one:** Sword of Dawn -> Dawn of the Xan; Sword of Dusk -> Dusk of the Xan.
- Source: https://www.ao-universe.com/guides/shadowlands/tradeskill-guides-5/weapon-3/sword-of-dawndusk
- Source: http://wiki.aodb.us/wiki/The_Beast

### Step 2 - Get a Xan Weapon Upgrade Device.

The Xan Weapon Upgrade Device is QL 300, NODROP/Unique and is consumed by the combine. It drops in the three Legacy of the Xan team instances entered from floor portals in the Central Hub playfield: The Alien Threat / 'Mitaar Hero' (portal at 350 x 410, boss Technomaster Sinuh), LoX: Ground Chief Vortexx (portal at 523 x 307), and The Xan 12-man (portal at 419 x 324, final boss Deranged Xan). Since patch 18.3 none of them need the old prerequisite quests. No drop rate is published. The combine itself needs NO tradeskill skill.

- **Drops:** Drops in all three Legacy of the Xan team instances. No drop rate is published on either allowed source.
- **Which one:** One device per weapon; it is consumed by the combine.
- Source: https://www.ao-universe.com/guides/legacy-of-the-xan/encounter-guides-2/the-alien-threat
- Source: https://www.ao-universe.com/guides/legacy-of-the-xan/encounter-guides-2/lox-ground-chief-vortexx

- Source: http://wiki.aodb.us/wiki/Xan_Weapon_Upgrade

### Step 3 - Combine: Xan Weapon Upgrade Device + base weapon = <Sin> of the Xan.

Done in the tradeskill window (Shift+T). NO tradeskill skill is required. The base weapon must be QL 299 or 300.

- **Which one:** Each Lord/Lady of <sin> becomes '<sin> of the Xan'; Sword of Dawn -> Dawn of the Xan and Sword of Dusk -> Dusk of the Xan. Relevant to Bureaucrat: Lord/Lady of Abandonment -> Abandonment of the Xan (1h Blunt); Lord/Lady of Envy -> Envy of the Xan (MG/SMG); Lord/Lady of Greed -> Greed of the Xan (Shotgun); Lord/Lady of Lust -> Lust of the Xan (Pistol); Lord/Lady of Wisdom -> Wisdom of the Xan (Martial Arts). (For skills this profession does not use: Anger, Angst, Chaos, Deceit, Gluttony, Hatred, Pride, Sloth; Lord/Lady of Wisdom -> Kuma Tonfa.) Angst additionally accepts High Lord of Angst; Anger/Envy/Angst additionally accept the matching Superior Rebuilt Perennium weapon.
- Source: http://wiki.aodb.us/wiki/Xan_Weapon_Upgrade
- Source: https://www.ao-universe.com/guides/legacy-of-the-xan/tradeskill-guides-2/weapons/xan-weapon-upgrade

### Step 4 - Optional final tier: Modified Xan Weapons.

A finished 'X of the Xan' plus one shop-bought component, QL 150 or higher, consumed. No tradeskill skill required.

- **Which one:** Gluttony of the Xan + Notum Enriched Nano Paste - 16x Layer = Bhan'Zor Hammer (2h Blunt). Abandonment of the Xan + Notum Enriched Nano Paste - 16x Layer = Kur'Ush Mallet (1h Blunt). Lust of the Xan + Nano Pylon = Peh'Wer Pistol, then Peh'Wer Pistol + Runtime Critical Area Analyzer = Troa'Ler Pistol. (Envy -> Rhat'Ata Gun and Angst -> Gan'Kar Rifle are MG/SMG and Rifle.) There is no documented modified version for Chaos, Pride, Hatred, Dawn or Dusk.
- Source: http://wiki.aodb.us/wiki/Modified_Xan_Weapons

## Deviations from the template classes

- 474 endgame models against the Soldier's 623 and the Doctor's 597, and the shape is lopsided: Pistol 128, 1h Blunt 94, MG-SMG 74, Shotgun 74, Ranged Energy 56, Martial Arts 30, Melee Energy 18. Only the Pistol column is a real endgame choice for a Crat; the rest are recorded because the class CAN wield them, at 3.2-4.0 IP.
- THE XAN LINE IS REACHABLE FOR FIVE OF THE SEVEN SKILLS AND NOT FOR RANGED ENERGY. Every Xan weapon starts as a Beast weapon named for a sin, and the sin fixes the skill. Of the Crat's seven skills the Beast supplies Lust (Pistol), Abandonment (1h Blunt), Envy (MG-SMG), Greed (Shotgun) and Wisdom (Martial Arts). Melee Energy has no sin weapon but has its own route - Sword of Dawn / Sword of Dusk, the only step in the whole line that needs a tradeskill (500 Weapon Smithing). RANGED ENERGY HAS NEITHER: a Bureaucrat wielding Ranged Energy has no Xan endgame at all.
- 'Lust of the Xan' is the Bureaucrat's headline weapon and its starting piece is not farmable on demand: Lord/Lady of Lust drops from The Beast in Pandemonium on a 9-hour respawn, and the upgrade device drops in the three Legacy-of-the-Xan Central Hub instances. Both halves are raid content, so the realistic solo-Crat endgame pistol is the Ofab or Dreadloch line, not Xan.
- 9 templates were EXCLUDED and each says why in _unverified.excludedModels: 4 GM/test templates whose skill requirement never scaled with QL (206720, 206722, 206723, 258345), 3 generic NPC weapons that top out at QL400 when no player item exceeds QL300 (43074, 44007, 45605), 1 player-unreachable template (295603, 2500 max damage at a skill requirement of 49999) and 1 template with no row in itemnames.sql (305998) whose name therefore could not be verified.
- Source coverage is 88 of 474 models (19%), in line with the Soldier (102 of 623) and the Doctor (89 of 597). The gap is the same one every class hits: local data has the item, the drop database that has the mob is blocked to automation, so the row says unknown instead of guessing.

## Data caveats

- Requirements, damage and gates are read at each model's TOP QL (statsReadAtQl) and scale down with QL.
- specials lists the special-attack skill requirements present in the item's ToWield criteria; the client decides at runtime which specials are actually offered.
- Damage figures are the item's base MinDamage/MaxDamage stats, before any character damage modifiers or add-damage.
